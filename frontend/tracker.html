Calculation Baselines

EcoTrack calculates carbon footprints using verified emission factor standards (kg CO₂e per unit):

Petrol Car 0.19 kg / km
Motorcycle 0.10 kg / km
City Bus 0.08 kg / km
Train / Rail 0.04 kg / km
Grid Electricity 0.82 kg / kWh
Meat Meal 3.50 kg / meal
Vegetarian Meal 1.50 kg / meal
