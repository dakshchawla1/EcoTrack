A practical tool for individual climate awareness

Most carbon accounting feels disconnected from everyday decisions. EcoTrack connects simple daily actions with measurable environmental data.

01

Log Everyday Habits

Keep a quick running record of your transport, home electricity consumption, diet, and purchases without tedious questionnaires.

02

Understand the Math

03

Four areas that shape your carbon footprint

Focusing on the everyday lifestyle choices that contribute most significantly to personal emissions.

Transport

Travel & Commute

Record vehicle commutes, motorcycle rides, bus trips, and train journeys. Compare the environmental efficiency of transit choices.

Car: ~0.19 kg CO₂/km Train: ~0.04 kg CO₂/km
Electricity

Home Power & Energy

Track monthly or weekly kilowatt-hours to monitor the carbon intensity of home cooling, heating, appliances, and lighting.

Average grid factor: ~0.82 kg CO₂/kWh
Food

Dietary Choices

Quantify the environmental differences between plant-based meals and animal products. Small dietary shifts yield noticeable reductions.

Plant-based: ~1.5 kg CO₂/meal
Shopping

Purchases & Goods

Account for the embodied carbon embedded in clothing, electronics, and manufactured items over their lifecycle.

Focus on reuse & longevity

Tools that keep you motivated and accountable

Everything you need to observe your footprint clearly without overwhelming complexity.

Personal Carbon Target

Set a realistic monthly ceiling in kilograms of CO₂. The dashboard visually tracks your cumulative usage so you stay under budget.

Automatically compares your emissions over the past 7 days against the preceding week to give you immediate, actionable feedback.

Practical sustainability milestones designed to encourage low-emission routines like taking public transit or trimming energy use.
