ABOUT ECOTRACK

What is EcoTrack?

A simple platform for understanding everyday carbon emissions.

A carbon footprint is an estimate of the greenhouse gas emissions connected to an activity.

EcoTrack lets you record some common activities from daily life and calculates an estimated carbon emission for them.

The aim is to make the information easier to understand and help users keep track of their activities over time.
